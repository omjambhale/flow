Intelligence was never built from a single sense.  A child learns to catch a ball not by watching it—by hearing it leave the bat, tracking it through space, feeling their feet shift, closing their fingers at exactly the right moment of resistance. Every sense, at once. This is how humans learn: through unified, multi-sensory experiences that compound into physical intelligence. Why should a robot learn any differently?

We capture human action across every sense simultaneously and convert it into the fused signal that physical AI learns from. Keeping intelligence real.

The multi-sensory platform for Physical AI.

Sound, sight, mobility, touch. One fused training signal from real world.

The problem

The internet was the dataset. That era is closing — and for physical AI, it never opened at all. There is no archive of a kitchen being cleaned. No dataset of a car being assembled by hands that know exactly how much force each joint requires. Physical AI needs structured, multi-sensory recordings of human activity in real environments. One synchronized, fused signal.

The solution

Transform human experience into episodic training signal for Physical AI

(01)

Diversity

20+ countries. Vietnam · Nigeria · Egypt · S. Korea · India · Brazil. The primary deployment markets for physical AI. Western-trained models have zero exposure to these environments.

(02)

Quality (Fusion Engine)

Four sensor streams (camera, IMU, audio, kinematics) synchronized at capture—not in post. Sound, sight, mobility, touch become one co-registered fused signal.

(03)

Provenance

Every data point ships with full traceability: contributor ID, environment, timestamp, hardware serial. Verified on-chain per contributor. This is the defensible layer physical AI data requires.

(04)

Complete Data Infrastructure

One integrated pipeline. We handle synchronization, validation, processing, QC, and benchmark evaluation—then ship training-ready data in MCAP, RLDS, and LeRobot v3.

The product stack

We are building the tools. For every stage.

Sourcing to shipping—every stage has a tool, every modality has a tool under it. Validation tools to catch failures invisible to the eye. Processing pipelines to remove noise. Labeling stacks for episodic data structure.

Partnership Agreement

Between Humynai Private Limited, 837/1 Binnamangala, 1st Stage 100 ft Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038 (the “Company”) and Kulkarni Field Ops, Pune, Maharashtra (the “Partner”).

1. Recitals

The Company collects, processes and delivers high-fidelity, multimodal datasets for the training of frontier AI and robotics systems. The Partner establishes relationships with manufacturing units, factories and warehouses (“Introduced Facilities”) and facilitates access to them for egocentric data collection, as described in each Statement of Work (“SOW”).

2. Scope of services and fees

The Partner is appointed as a non-exclusive intermediary to identify and introduce suitable facilities, facilitate access, ensure permissions and consents are in place before recording, and provide on-ground coordination during collection sessions. The Company pays the Partner the Fee specified in each SOW; this is the Partner's sole compensation.

3. Term and termination

One year from the Effective Date. Either party may terminate for convenience with one month's written notice, or immediately for material breach not cured within 60 days.

4. Intellectual property

All data collected and everything derived from it (“Project IP”) is the sole property of the Company from the moment of creation. The Partner has no claim to any models, products or revenue built on it.

5. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information private, uses it only to perform this agreement, and shares it strictly on a need-to-know basis.

8. Obligations of the Partner

Collection at every facility follows the SOW's validation criteria: brief all personnel before each session, set up and operate equipment correctly, monitor sessions and correct deviations, and suspend collection where criteria can't be met. The Company may reject data that fails the criteria; no fee is payable on rejected data.

SOW 1 · Equipment handling and responsibility

The Partner is responsible for the safe handling and operational custody of all devices during deployment. Physical damage from operator or site mishandling, and any loss of assets, is borne by the Partner and settled at asset cost in the next payment cycle. Natural or technical failure is handled by the Company. SD cards are used only for collection and transfer; no footage may be retained or copied by the Partner.

SOW 1 · Fees

Billing is strictly against validated hours, on a 15-day payment cycle. An hour is valid when lighting is bright and consistent, both hands are visible for at least 80% of footage, the camera is stable, and 70–80% of footage shows genuine interaction with real objects.

11. Governing law

Laws of India; disputes resolved by arbitration seated in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
